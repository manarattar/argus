# ARGUS Technology Risk Assessment Standard (TRA)

> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION POLICY.** Written for the ARGUS demonstration
> corpus. Not the policy of any institution and of no regulatory standing.

Version 1.4 | Owner: Technology Risk | Effective 1 January 2026
Subordinate to the Counterparty Risk Framework (CRF)

<!-- page: 1 -->

## 1. Application

**Clause reference: TRA 1.1**

This standard applies to counterparties operating technology environments whose
disruption would materially affect their ability to perform, including all
counterparties operating industrial control or operational technology systems.

## 2. Control Assessment

**Clause reference: TRA 2.1**

Where an independent assessment of the counterparty's technology controls
exists, the assessment must record the assessor, the date, the scope and the
resulting maturity rating.

**Clause reference: TRA 2.2**

A control maturity rating below 3.0 on a five-point scale is classified as below
expectation and is a review trigger.

**Clause reference: TRA 2.3**

Where no independent assessment exists, the absence must be recorded as an
information gap under CRF 6.2 and the assessment must not draw a favourable
conclusion on technology risk from management assertion alone.

## 3. Segregation of Environments

**Clause reference: TRA 3.1**

The absence of network segmentation between corporate information technology and
operational technology environments is classified as a high-severity control
weakness.

**Clause reference: TRA 3.2**

Where a high-severity weakness has been remediated, the assessment must record
whether the remediation has been independently verified by re-test.

## 4. Detection and Incident History

<!-- page: 2 -->

**Clause reference: TRA 4.1**

A statement that no security incidents have occurred must be assessed against
the counterparty's detection capability during the same period. Where
centralised logging or adequate retention was absent, an absence of reported
incidents must be recorded as weak evidence and must not be relied upon.

**Clause reference: TRA 4.2**

Log retention of fewer than 180 days is classified as below expectation for
counterparties within the scope of this standard.

## 5. Third-Party Technology Risk

**Clause reference: TRA 5.1**

The assessment must record whether the counterparty operates a process for
assessing the security posture of suppliers holding network connectivity or data
access.

**Clause reference: TRA 5.2**

Where suppliers hold such access and no assessment process exists, this is
classified as a potential breach of this standard and must be escalated under
CRF 7.2.

## 6. Remediation Credit

**Clause reference: TRA 6.1**

Remediation verified by independent re-test may be credited in full.
Remediation asserted by management without independent verification may be
credited only in part, and the distinction must be stated in the assessment.
