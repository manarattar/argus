# ARGUS Financial Resilience Standard (FRS)

> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION POLICY.** Written for the ARGUS demonstration
> corpus. Not the policy of any institution and of no regulatory standing.

Version 2.1 | Owner: Group Credit Risk | Effective 1 January 2026
Subordinate to the Counterparty Risk Framework (CRF)

<!-- page: 1 -->

## 1. Application

**Clause reference: FRS 1.1**

This standard applies where a counterparty is classified as having elevated
financial leverage under CRF 3.2, or where a review trigger under CRF 3.3 has
been raised.

## 2. Leverage

**Clause reference: FRS 2.1**

Leverage must be assessed on both a current and a forward-looking basis. A
current position within covenant is not sufficient where the forward position
under the counterparty's own downside scenario approaches the covenant limit.

**Clause reference: FRS 2.2**

Covenant headroom of less than 0.25x on any tested measure, under the
counterparty's own stated downside scenario, must be recorded as a specific
risk with a severity of at least Major.

**Clause reference: FRS 2.3**

Where leverage has increased as a result of capital investment, the assessment
must consider whether the investment is commissioned and revenue-generating, and
whether the revenue underpinning it is contracted.

## 3. Earnings Quality

<!-- page: 2 -->

**Clause reference: FRS 3.1**

Revenue growth must be assessed alongside margin trend. Revenue growth
accompanied by margin compression across two or more consecutive periods must be
recorded and explained, and must not be presented as unqualified evidence of
improving financial condition.

**Clause reference: FRS 3.2**

Where margin compression is attributed by the counterparty to non-recurring
factors, the assessment must state whether that attribution is supported by
evidence or is management assertion only.

## 4. Liquidity and Working Capital

**Clause reference: FRS 4.1**

Available liquidity must be assessed as the sum of cash and undrawn committed
facilities, expressed relative to monthly operating expenditure.

**Clause reference: FRS 4.2**

A deterioration in the cash conversion cycle of more than 15 days across the
review period must be recorded and explained.

**Clause reference: FRS 4.3**

Where inventory has increased materially, the assessment must distinguish
between a deliberate strategic buffer and a deterioration in inventory turnover.
The distinction changes the risk implication and must not be left ambiguous.

## 5. Scenario Analysis

<!-- page: 3 -->

**Clause reference: FRS 5.1**

The assessment must record the counterparty's own downside scenario, its stated
assumptions and its resulting covenant position.

**Clause reference: FRS 5.2**

Where the counterparty's downside scenario depends on discretionary mitigating
actions, those actions must be identified and their feasibility assessed
separately from the scenario outcome.
