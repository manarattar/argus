# ARGUS Governance Oversight Standard (GOS)

> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION POLICY.** Written for the ARGUS demonstration
> corpus. Not the policy of any institution and of no regulatory standing.

Version 3.0 | Owner: Group Risk | Effective 1 January 2026
Subordinate to the Counterparty Risk Framework (CRF)

<!-- page: 1 -->

## 1. Board Composition

**Clause reference: GOS 1.1**

The assessment must record the number of directors, the number who are
independent, and the nominating party for each non-independent director.

**Clause reference: GOS 1.2**

A board on which independent directors constitute less than one third of
membership is classified as having limited independent oversight.

**Clause reference: GOS 1.3**

Where the chair is nominated by a controlling shareholder and independent
directors constitute less than one third of the board, the assessment must
consider whether material board decisions in the review period were taken
without independent challenge, and must identify those decisions.

## 2. Committees

**Clause reference: GOS 2.1**

An audit committee is expected to meet no fewer than four times per financial
year. Fewer than four meetings must be recorded as a deficiency.

**Clause reference: GOS 2.2**

The chair of the audit committee is expected to be independent. Where this is
not the case for any part of the review period, the period during which it was
not the case must be recorded.

**Clause reference: GOS 2.3**

The absence of a risk committee must be recorded together with the arrangements
in place for board consideration of risk.

## 3. Internal Control

<!-- page: 2 -->

**Clause reference: GOS 3.1**

The assessment must record whether an internal audit function exists and, if
not, what compensating arrangements are in place.

**Clause reference: GOS 3.2**

Significant deficiencies identified by the external auditor must be recorded
individually, with their remediation status and the date first raised.

**Clause reference: GOS 3.3**

A significant deficiency that has remained open across two consecutive audit
cycles is classified as a persistent control deficiency and must be recorded as
a specific risk. Deficiencies bearing on segregation of duties in payment or
procurement processes carry the highest weighting under this clause.

## 4. Remediation

**Clause reference: GOS 4.1**

Where a counterparty has announced governance remediation, the assessment must
distinguish between measures implemented and demonstrated in operation, measures
implemented but not yet demonstrated, and measures announced but not implemented.

**Clause reference: GOS 4.2**

Announced but undemonstrated remediation may be recorded as a mitigating factor.
It may not be treated as resolving the underlying deficiency, and a rating may
not be reduced on the basis of remediation that has not been demonstrated in
operation.

**Clause reference: GOS 4.3**

Where remediation is recent and unproven, a shortened review cycle is the
expected response in preference to either an unqualified favourable conclusion
or a conclusion that disregards the remediation.
