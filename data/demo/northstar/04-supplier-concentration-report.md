# Northstar Manufacturing B.V. — Supply Base Assessment

> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT.** Prepared for the ARGUS demonstration
> corpus. Northstar Manufacturing B.V. does not exist.

Prepared by: Supply Chain Risk Team
Date: 2 April 2026
Basis: procurement data FY2025, supplier questionnaires, site visit Ostrava
(February 2026)

## 1. Supply Base Profile

Northstar sources from 214 active suppliers across 19 countries. Direct
materials spend in FY2025 was EUR 78.4 million.

| Measure | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active suppliers | 231 | 224 | 214 |
| Direct materials spend (EUR m) | 54.1 | 66.2 | 78.4 |
| Top 10 suppliers, % of spend | 64% | 68% | 71% |
| Top 3 suppliers, % of spend | 34% | 37% | 41% |
| Single-sourced components | 3 | 2 | 2 |

Supplier count has fallen while spend has risen. Concentration in the top ten
has increased by seven percentage points over three years. Management describes
this as an intentional consolidation programme aimed at improving commercial
terms and quality consistency.

## 2. Single-Source Positions

<!-- page: 2 -->

Two components remain single-sourced. Both are used in the company's principal
product family, which generated 68% of FY2025 revenue.

### 2.1 Copper alloy extrusion (component NM-4412)

Supplier: Fonderia Alpina S.p.A., Lecco, Italy. Supplying Northstar since 2014.

- FY2025 spend: EUR 9.2 million (11.7% of direct materials).
- Alternative qualified sources: none.
- Management estimate of requalification lead time: 9–14 months, driven by
  customer-mandated PPAP requirements on the end platform.
- Supplier financial standing: assessed as stable. Most recent filed accounts
  (FY2024) show revenue of EUR 61 million and net cash. No adverse credit
  events recorded.
- Geographic exposure: single production site, Lecco. The site is not in a
  designated flood or seismic risk zone.
- Contractual position: rolling twelve-month supply agreement, ninety days'
  notice of termination by either party.

### 2.2 Thermal interface material (component NM-7730)

<!-- page: 3 -->

Supplier: Hanseo Advanced Materials Co. Ltd, Cheonan, South Korea. Supplying
Northstar since 2019.

- FY2025 spend: EUR 4.8 million (6.1% of direct materials).
- Alternative qualified sources: none. Two alternatives were evaluated in 2023
  and neither met thermal cycling specification.
- Management estimate of requalification lead time: 12–14 months, and requires
  end-customer approval.
- Supplier financial standing: limited visibility. Hanseo is privately held and
  does not publish accounts. Northstar has not obtained a credit report on this
  supplier. A supplier questionnaire was issued in November 2025 and had not
  been returned as at the date of this assessment.
- Geographic exposure: single production site.
- Contractual position: purchase-order basis only. There is no supply agreement.

The Hanseo position is the weaker of the two. Northstar has a single-source
dependency, with no contract, on a supplier whose financial condition it has not
assessed, for a component whose requalification would take over a year and
require customer approval.

## 3. Mitigating Measures in Place

<!-- page: 4 -->

The company has taken several concrete steps that materially reduce the
near-term exposure described above.

- **Buffer inventory.** Northstar holds 71 days of inventory overall (FY2024:
  54 days). For the two single-sourced components specifically, the company
  holds strategic buffer stock of 118 days (NM-4412) and 134 days (NM-7730),
  established during 2025.
- **Dual-site capability.** Following the Ostrava expansion, machining
  operations can be performed at either site for the majority of the principal
  product family, reducing exposure to a single internal facility.
- **Supplier audits.** Fonderia Alpina was audited on site by Northstar quality
  in June 2025 with no major findings. Hanseo has not been audited on site;
  the last quality review was conducted remotely in 2022.
- **Requalification programme.** In January 2026 the company initiated a
  programme to qualify a second source for NM-7730, with a target completion of
  Q3 2027. A candidate supplier has been identified and sample testing has
  begun.

The buffer stock positions are the most significant of these. At 118 and 134
days respectively, they would absorb a total supply interruption for roughly
four months, which is a meaningful proportion of the stated requalification
lead time but not all of it.

## 4. Assessment

<!-- page: 5 -->

Supply base concentration has increased consistently over the review period and
two single-source positions remain in the company's most important product
family.

The Fonderia Alpina position is well-managed on the evidence available: a
long-standing relationship, a financially sound supplier, a recent on-site
audit, a contract, and 118 days of buffer stock.

The Hanseo position is not well-managed. The combination of no contract, no
financial assessment, no recent on-site audit and a requalification path
exceeding twelve months is a genuine weakness, only partly offset by the buffer
stock and the requalification programme now under way.

It would be wrong to characterise Northstar's supply chain as unmanaged. The
buffer stock programme and the Ostrava dual-site capability are substantive
mitigations that were funded and executed. The specific weakness is narrower
than the overall concentration figures suggest, and it is concentrated in one
supplier relationship.

## 5. Recommendations

1. Obtain a credit assessment of Hanseo Advanced Materials within 60 days.
2. Conduct an on-site audit of Hanseo before the end of 2026.
3. Put a written supply agreement in place with Hanseo, or accelerate the
   second-source qualification.
4. Report buffer stock coverage for single-sourced components to the board
   quarterly.
