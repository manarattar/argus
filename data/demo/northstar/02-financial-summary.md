## 4. Covenant Position

<!-- page: 4 -->

Facility agreement dated 12 March 2025. Tested semi-annually at 30 June and
31 December.

| Covenant | Limit | Jun 2025 | Dec 2025 | Headroom at Dec 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Net leverage | ≤ 3.25x | 1.9x | 2.40x | 0.85x |
| Interest cover | ≥ 3.00x | 6.2x | 4.10x | 1.10x |

Both covenants were met at both test dates in 2025. Headroom narrowed between
the June and December test dates on both measures.

Applying the company's own downside scenario (loss of the second-largest
customer at contract expiry in June 2026 with no replacement volume), net
leverage would rise to an estimated 3.1x by the June 2027 test date on
management's projections — inside the covenant, but with headroom of
approximately 0.15x.

## 6. Analyst Commentary

<!-- page: 6 -->

The financial picture is genuinely mixed and should not be characterised in one
direction.

Supporting a favourable view: revenue growth has been sustained across three
years; the order book provides visibility; both covenants were met with
headroom at the most recent test; liquidity improved in absolute and relative
terms; the largest customer relationship is contracted to end-2027; the
increase in borrowing funded a capacity expansion that is now commissioned and
carries contracted revenue behind it.

Supporting an unfavourable view: leverage rose 2.5x in a single year; margin has
compressed in each of three years, so growth is being bought at declining
returns; concentration has increased every year and 23% of revenue sits behind
a contract expiring in under three months without a signed renewal; the
company's own downside scenario leaves covenant headroom of roughly 0.15x by
mid-2027.

The two views are not in conflict. The company's current position is sound and
its forward position is more sensitive to a single commercial outcome — the
June 2026 renewal — than it was at any point in the prior three years.
